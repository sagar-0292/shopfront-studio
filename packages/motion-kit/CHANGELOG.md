# Motion kit releases

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-25
First release. Scroll reveals (8 styles), split-text headlines, parallax, pinned horizontal scroll, marquee, magnetic buttons, tilt cards, custom cursor with labels, progress bar, auto-hiding header, smooth scrolling, aurora/particles/waves backgrounds, video hero, 3D objects (ring, gem, knot, blob, cup, orbit, stack) and client .glb models. Respects reduced motion, scales down on phones and low-power devices, pauses off-screen, and includes a visitor "Pause animations" button.
