# Design kit releases

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-26
First release. Four design directions — Editorial luxury, Bold & vibrant, Dark & cinematic and Warm & crafted — each with its own fonts, colours, spacing and signature details, and seventeen ready sections (heroes in four layouts, scrolling marquee, statement, product grids, categories, sideways-scrolling story, features, gallery, restaurant menu with veg/non-veg marks, booking, reviews, numbers, questions, call to action, contact with map and opening hours, shop and wishlist). Pages are built from a checked page description; custom colours must pass readability checks.
