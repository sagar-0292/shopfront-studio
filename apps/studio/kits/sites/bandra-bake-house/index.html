Little questions

How long does sourdough keep?

Three to four days wrapped in a cloth. Slice and freeze it after that — it toasts beautifully from frozen.

Do you make eggless or vegan bakes?

Most cakes and cookies can be made eggless. Our sourdough, focaccia and pav are vegan.

How much notice do you need for a cake?

Three days for most cakes, a week for tiered or sculpted ones.
