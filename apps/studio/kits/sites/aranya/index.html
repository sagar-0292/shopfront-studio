Good to know

Questions, answered

Is your gold hallmarked?

Yes. Every gold piece carries the BIS hallmark and a unique HUID number you can verify on the BIS Care app.

Can you remake my grandmother's jewellery?

Often, yes. Bring it to a viewing; we will tell you honestly what can be kept, reset or melted, and give you a written estimate.

How long does a commission take?

Usually five to eight weeks, depending on the stones. We send photographs at each stage.

Do you deliver outside Mumbai?

Yes, fully insured, anywhere in India. Or collect from the atelier.
