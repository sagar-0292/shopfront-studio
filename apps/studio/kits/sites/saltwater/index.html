Before you come

How do we get there from Mumbai?

Take the ferry from the Gateway of India to Mandwa (about an hour). We'll pick you up from the jetty. By road it's about three hours.

Are children and pets welcome?

Children of all ages, yes. Pets by arrangement: tell us when you book.

Can we book the whole house?

Yes, for up to ten guests. Ask us on WhatsApp for dates and the whole-house rate.
