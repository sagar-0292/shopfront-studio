Before you come

Is there parking?

Yes, paid parking inside Kamala Mills, and valet from 8 pm on weekends.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

About half the menu is vegetarian, marked with a green dot. Our kitchen cooks vegetarian food on separate grills.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. We'd just like you to be comfortable.
