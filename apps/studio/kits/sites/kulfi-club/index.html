Good questions

How do the boxes stay frozen?

We pack them in insulated bags with dry ice. They stay frozen for four hours, so put them straight in the freezer when they arrive.

Is your kulfi eggless?

Yes. Everything we make is vegetarian and eggless. The rose lollies contain white chocolate.

Do you deliver outside Mumbai?

Not yet. We deliver across Mumbai and Thane, free on orders over ₹799.
