Questions?

How long do the sweets stay fresh?

Milk sweets are best within 3 days and should go in the fridge. Kaju katli, soan papdi and namkeen keep for 2 to 3 weeks.

Do you deliver outside Mumbai?

Dry sweets, namkeen and gift boxes ship across India in 2 to 5 days. Milk sweets are Mumbai-only.

Can I pay by UPI or cash?

Yes. Pay by any UPI app, cash on delivery for orders up to ₹5,000, or order on WhatsApp.
