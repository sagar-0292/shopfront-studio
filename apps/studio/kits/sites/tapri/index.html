How we brew

How a cutting is made

  1. 01

    Four minutes

    Boil it hard

    Assam CTC, water and crushed ginger go on a high flame. Four minutes, no shortcuts, no tea bags.

  2. 02

    Twice

    Pull it high

    Milk goes in and we pour from a height, twice, until the chai froths and the spices open up.

  3. 03

    Half a glass

    Cut it in half

    That's a cutting: strong enough to wake you, small enough to have two. ₹20 a glass.

  4. 04

    Or in clay

    Take it in a kulhad

    Ask for kulhad chai and it comes in a hand-thrown clay cup that adds its own earthy smell.

Good questions

Do you deliver chai?

Hot chai doesn't travel well, so we only deliver our blends and kulhad sets. Delivery is free across Mumbai on orders over ₹499.

Can you do chai for an office or a wedding?

Yes. We bring a counter, a brewer and kulhads for 50 to 500 people. Message us on WhatsApp with the date.

Is there sugar-free chai?

Ask at the counter. We brew it without sugar and you add jaggery or nothing at all.
